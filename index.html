01 / THE FILM

A JOURNEY
BEYOND TIME.

Interstellar is a 2014 science-fiction epic directed by Christopher Nolan. It follows Cooper, a former pilot turned farmer, who joins a mission beyond the limits of ordinary human space travel in search of a future for humanity.

The story combines family, exploration, relativity, black holes, wormholes, survival and the strange relationship between time and gravity.

03 / GARGANTUA

ENTER THE
BLACK HOLE.

Gargantua is the enormous rotating black hole encountered by the expedition. Its powerful gravity bends the paths of light and strongly affects the passage of time near it.

The film's black-hole imagery was developed with physicist Kip Thorne and was based on calculations involving general relativity.

04 / COSMIC SCIENCE

THE SCIENCE

Interstellar uses real scientific concepts as major parts of its story. Some ideas are established physics, while others are speculative or dramatically simplified.

🕳️

BLACK HOLES

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity becomes extremely strong. The boundary around it is called the event horizon.

🌀

WORMHOLES

A wormhole is a hypothetical shortcut through spacetime connecting distant regions. Traversable wormholes remain speculative.

⏳

TIME DILATION

Relativity predicts that clocks can run at different rates depending on motion and gravitational environment.

🌌

SPACETIME

Einstein's relativity treats space and time as connected parts of spacetime, which can be curved by mass and energy.

🌊

TIDAL FORCES

Differences in gravitational pull across an object can stretch or compress it. Near compact massive objects these effects can become extreme.

✨

RELATIVITY

General relativity describes gravity geometrically: matter and energy affect the geometry of spacetime.

10 / MISSION PUZZLES

Three small space-themed challenges. Solve them like a mission specialist.

PUZZLE 01

THE LIGHT SIGNAL

A signal flashes every 3 seconds. You observe 5 flashes after the first one.

How many seconds passed between the first and fifth flash?

Solution: There are four intervals between five flashes. 4 × 3 = 12 seconds.
PUZZLE 02

THE PLANET CODE

Decode this sequence:

2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → ?

What number comes next?

Solution: Each number is multiplied by 2. 16 × 2 = 32.
PUZZLE 03

TIME DILATION

Imagine a fictional planet where 1 local hour equals 7 Earth years. How many Earth years correspond to 3 local hours?

Solution: 3 × 7 = 21 Earth years.
PUZZLE 04

GRAVITY MESSAGE

Complete the pattern:

SPACE → TIME
MASS → GRAVITY
ENERGY → ?

Think about Einstein's physics.

Solution: The intended answer is SPACETIME. In relativity, mass-energy influences the geometry of spacetime.
