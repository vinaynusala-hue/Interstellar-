THE COSMOS

THE UNIVERSE

The universe contains everything that exists: space, time, matter, energy, galaxies, stars, planets, black holes and phenomena that we are still learning to understand.

🌌 Galaxies

Vast structures containing millions, billions or even trillions of stars.

⭐ Stars

Giant spheres of plasma powered by nuclear fusion deep within their cores.

🌑 Dark Matter

Invisible matter whose presence is inferred from its gravitational effects.

⚡ Dark Energy

A mysterious component associated with the accelerating expansion of the universe.

ORBITAL SYSTEM

WORLDS

Planets are worlds shaped by gravity, chemistry, geology and their environments. Some orbit stars; others may travel through space without a parent star.

EXTREME GRAVITY

BLACK HOLE

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity becomes so strong that escaping from inside its event horizon is impossible.

SPACETIME

WORMHOLE

Wormholes are hypothetical structures in spacetime that are often imagined as shortcuts between distant regions of the universe.

THE FOURTH DIMENSION

TIME

Space and time are connected into spacetime. Relativity shows that measurements of time and distance depend on motion and gravity.
