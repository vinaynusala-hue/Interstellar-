GARGANTUA

THE BLACK
HOLE

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity becomes extraordinarily strong. In the movie, Gargantua is depicted with an accretion disk and strong gravitational lensing.

The bright disk appears distorted because light travels through strongly curved spacetime around the black hole.

RELATIVITY

TIME IS
NOT ABSOLUTE

One of the most famous scientific concepts explored by Interstellar is gravitational time dilation.

Δτ = Δt √(1 − 2GM/rc²)

This simplified expression illustrates how the passage of proper time can differ near a massive object in a simplified Schwarzschild spacetime.

The closer an observer is to a sufficiently massive object, the stronger the relativistic effects can become.

MISSION CONTROL

CAN YOU
SOLVE IT?

Three small space-themed puzzles. Think like an astronaut, scientist and navigator.

PUZZLE 01

THE SIGNAL

Decode this sequence:

1 — 3 — 5 — 7 — ?

SOLUTION: The sequence increases by 2. Therefore the next number is 9.
PUZZLE 02

TIME DILATION

Suppose 1 hour near an extreme gravitational environment corresponds to 7 years elsewhere.

2 hours = ?

SOLUTION: 2 × 7 = 14 years. This is only a fictional puzzle ratio.
PUZZLE 03

PLANET ORBIT

A planet completes one orbit every 10 units. How many complete orbits occur in 50 units?

SOLUTION: 50 ÷ 10 = 5 complete orbits.
