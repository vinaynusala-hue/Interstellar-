07 / INTERSTELLAR PUZZLE

CAN YOU
SOLVE IT?

MISSION CODE

Cooper receives a mysterious sequence:

20 — 9 — 13 — 5

Convert each number into its corresponding English alphabet letter.

How to solve

Use A=1, B=2, C=3 ... Z=26. Therefore: 20=T, 9=I, 13=M, 5=E. The hidden word is TIME.
